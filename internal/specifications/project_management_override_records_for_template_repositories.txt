Project-Management Override Records for Template Repositories
=============================================================

Scope
-----
Define how a repository that serves as a reusable process template may track
its own maintenance work without polluting the clean template records that
downstream projects are meant to copy.

Problem Statement
-----------------
Template repositories need live backlog, task, bug, and completion records
for their own maintenance. Writing those records directly into the template
files makes the template look pre-populated and reduces its value as a clean
starting point for downstream projects. Bug-resolution records also need a
stable destination and enough structure to preserve why the bug existed and
how it was fixed.

Requirements
------------
- A template repository may define repository-local project-management record
  locations under `internal/overrides/`.
- `internal/README.md` must explain that material in and below `internal/` is
  intended for development of the submodule itself.
- When `internal/overrides/README.txt` exists, it is authoritative for the
  repository's live project-management record locations.
- The override tree must provide mirrored files for backlog, in-progress,
  completed, deferred, AI-human request, and proposal and bug tracking.
- Proposal tracking must provide `approved/`, `rejected/`, `deferred/`, and
  `under-review/` status directories under `proposals/`.
- Bug lifecycle tracking must use `known-bugs.txt`, `bugs-in-progress.txt`,
  and `resolved-bugs.txt`, plus the detailed-record status directories
  `open/`, `in-progress/`, and `closed/` under `bugs/`.
- Cleared bugs must move into `resolved-bugs.txt` rather than a generic
  closed-state file.
- Each resolved bug entry must include the recorded resolution timestamp, a
  short symptom summary, `Root cause:` analysis, and `Resolution:` notes.
- The starter files under `templates/project-management/` must remain clean
  template examples unless the template text or examples themselves are being
  revised.
- Maintenance work on the template itself must still be tracked, but the
  live records for that work must be written into the override files.
- Human- and AI-facing workflow guidance must explain the override precedence
  so operators use the correct files during normal maintenance.

Non-Goals
---------
- Removing starter templates under `templates/` without replacement.
- Preventing intentional edits to template wording or examples.
- Requiring downstream projects to adopt the override mechanism when they do
  not need clean-template preservation.
