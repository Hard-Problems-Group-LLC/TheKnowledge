Knack Authoring Guidance Allows Project-Specific Prose
======================================================

Status
------
Open.

Date Opened
-----------
2026-03-25T15:58:54-07:00

Scope
-----
This is a TheKnowledge documentation bug affecting knack authoring guidance
for consuming projects that keep additional knacks in their own top-level
`knacks/` tree.

Summary
-------
The current knack documentation makes the storage location clear but not the
content rule. It says consuming projects may keep "project-specific or third-
party knacks" in their own `knacks/` directory, but it does not clearly say
that knack prose should still stay generalized and potentially reusable as
general-purpose guidance.

Observed Failure Mode
---------------------
In this session, that wording gap led directly to local knack files written as
project-named guidance documents. They were stored in the right place, but the
content read like repository-specific notes rather than generalized knacks
that could later be merged into stock TheKnowledge or reused elsewhere.

Why The Current Wording Was Not Enough
--------------------------------------
- `knacks/authoring-guide.md` says consuming projects may keep "project-
  specific or third-party knacks" in the consuming project's own top-level
  `knacks/` directory.
- `knacks/README.md` repeats that consuming projects may keep proprietary or
  third-party knacks in their own `knacks/` directory.
- Neither document says clearly enough that "project-specific" refers to
  where the knack is stored and who currently has access to it, not to whether
  the prose itself should name the project or hard-code project-specific
  architecture.

Intended Rule Clarified By Operator
-----------------------------------
Project-local knacks are "project-specific" only in the repository-management
sense: they are available to that project and may not have been merged back
into stock TheKnowledge yet. Their content should still be generalized and
potentially reusable as general-purpose guidance.

Impact
------
- Contributors can reasonably infer that a project-local knack is supposed to
  read like project-specific notes.
- General-purpose knowledge becomes harder to reuse and harder to upstream.
- The distinction between a knack and an internal project memo becomes blurry.

Recommended Documentation Change
--------------------------------
Update the knack authoring guidance to say explicitly that:

1. consuming projects may keep additional knacks in their own `knacks/`
   directory;
2. those knacks may remain local because they are new, proprietary, or too
   obscure for stock TheKnowledge; but
3. knack prose should still stay generalized, reusable, and free of project-
   named framing unless the document is intentionally something other than a
   knack.

Why This Matters
----------------
Knacks are supposed to package reusable know-how. Clarifying that locality is
about storage and distribution, not about writing project-specific prose,
would keep consuming-project knack libraries more reusable and make upstreaming
new guidance easier.
