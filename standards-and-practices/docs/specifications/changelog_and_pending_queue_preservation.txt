Changelog and Pending-Queue Preservation
========================================

Scope
-----
Define the relationship between durable changelogs, completed-task records,
and `pending-commit-changes.txt` in TheKnowledge-managed repositories.

Problem Statement
-----------------
The pending-commit queue is intentionally short lived. It feeds useful commit
body text into the standardized commit helper and is cleared after a
successful local commit. Repositories that need durable release or
operator-facing history therefore need a separate changelog policy so notable
entries are not lost when the queue is truncated.

Requirements
------------
- `pending-commit-changes.txt` remains short-lived commit-body input, not a
  durable changelog.
- `completed-tasks.txt`, bug records, and proposal records remain operational
  traceability records, not release notes.
- Repositories with durable user-facing, operator-facing, or release-facing
  history needs should keep a top-level `CHANGELOG.md`.
- When a changelog exists, it should keep an `## [Unreleased]` section and
  use clear categories such as `Added`, `Changed`, `Fixed`, `Security`, and
  `Removed` as needed.
- Before running the standardized commit helper, maintainers should copy or
  summarize notable pending-queue entries into `CHANGELOG.md` when those
  entries affect durable release or operator history.
- Internal-only repositories with no durable release-history need may omit
  `CHANGELOG.md`, but they must not treat the pending queue as durable
  history.
- Any future helper-driven changelog sync must be explicit and reviewable; it
  must not guess release-note categories from arbitrary prose.

Non-Goals
---------
- Require changelogs in every repository.
- Replace project-management history files.
- Design a full release automation pipeline.
